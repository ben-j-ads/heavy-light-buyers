How it works

The model

Purchases per person in a period follow a negative binomial distribution, the same one the banana chart draws.

P(0) = (r / (r + m))^r P(k+1) = P(k) x (r + k) / (k + 1) x m / (r + m)

Drop the people who bought nothing, rank buyers from most purchases to fewest, and add up their purchases. Buyers with the same count are treated as one block and split evenly when a slice cuts through them.

Read this before you quote a number

This is a model of a typical category, not your data. Real categories vary, and the shape depends on how long the period is.

Heavy and light are about the period you pick. A person who buys once a year is light in a year and invisible in a week.

It describes the category. One brand's buyers are lighter than the category's buyers, because most of a brand's customers also buy other brands.
